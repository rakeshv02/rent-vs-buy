What is the Rent vs Buy Calculator?

The Rent vs Buy Calculator compares the total cost of renting a home against buying one over the years you plan to stay, so you can see which option leaves you financially better off. It weighs mortgage costs and home appreciation against rent and the returns you'd earn investing your deposit.

How it works

Buying costs include the deposit, mortgage interest, property taxes, maintenance, and transaction fees, offset by the equity you build and any appreciation. Renting costs the rent (which typically grows each year) but frees up your deposit to invest. The calculator projects both paths and finds the "breakeven" horizon: buying wins once total buying cost minus home value gain is lower than total rent minus investment gains.

Example 1 — Short stay. On a home where fees and interest dominate the first few years, staying only 3 years often makes renting cheaper because purchase costs aren't recovered yet.

Example 2 — Long stay. Holding the same property 10 years, with 3% annual appreciation and rising rents, usually tips the balance toward buying as equity and appreciation accumulate.

When to use it

  • Deciding whether to keep renting or purchase your first home.
  • Estimating how long you'd need to stay for buying to pay off.
  • Testing how interest rates or appreciation change the outcome.
  • Comparing markets with very different price-to-rent ratios.

Why does how long I stay matter so much?

Buying has large upfront costs; the longer you stay, the more time appreciation and equity have to outweigh them, which is why short stays often favour renting.
